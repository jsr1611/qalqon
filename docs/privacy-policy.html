Privacy Policy — Aqlli Qalqon

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Aqlli Qalqon (“the app”) is an on-device app locker for shared family phones, developed by Jumanazar Said Ruzim. This policy explains how the app handles information. In short: the app collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device.

Data we collect

None. Aqlli Qalqon does not collect, store on our servers, or transmit any personal or usage data. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs. The app has no account system and requires no sign-in. Everything it does happens locally on your device.

Information processed on your device

  • Your password and recovery code are stored only on your device, in the app’s private storage. The password is kept as a salted hash, never in plain text, and never leaves the device.
  • Which apps you choose to lock is stored only on your device.
  • The name of the app currently in the foreground is read in real time, solely to decide whether to show the password screen. It is used momentarily and never stored, logged, or sent anywhere.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Accessibility Service — used only to detect which app has come to the foreground so the app can show the password screen before a protected app opens. It does not read, collect, or transmit screen content, text, or any personal information.
  • Query installed apps — to show you a list of the apps on the device so you can pick which ones to lock. This list stays on the device.
  • Device administrator (optional) — if you enable it, prevents the app from being uninstalled without the parent password. It is not used to collect any data.
  • VPN / local DNS filter (optional, off by default) — if you turn it on, a local, on-device filter blocks selected websites. No traffic is sent to us or any third party; nothing is logged.
  • Biometrics (optional) — fingerprint/face verification is handled entirely by Android; the app never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Notifications / foreground service — to keep the protection running reliably.

Children

Aqlli Qalqon is a tool for parents and guardians to manage a shared device. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Data sharing and sale

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data, because we do not collect any.
